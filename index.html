FAQ

Common questions

Do you work on residential projects?

Our focus is commercial and multi-unit work, but we do glass railings and select residential projects. Send us the details and we'll let you know.

How fast can you board up a broken storefront?

Call 778-302-4774 right away. We prioritize emergency board-up calls and will give you a realistic arrival time on the phone.

Can you work from architectural drawings?

Yes. Send drawings or specs with your quote request. We prepare shop drawings for review before anything is ordered.

Are your glass railings code compliant?

Railings are designed and installed to meet BC Building Code guard requirements for the location. We confirm heights and loads at site measure.

What areas do you serve?

Vancouver, Burnaby, Richmond, Surrey, the North Shore, Coquitlam, New Westminster, Delta, Langley and the surrounding Lower Mainland.
